Data Processing Addendum - StartupAI Labs

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

1. Scope
This DPA governs processing of personal data on behalf of the Customer.

2. Roles
Customer acts as Controller. StartupAI Labs acts as Processor.

3. Processing Instructions
Processor will process personal data only on documented instructions from Controller.

4. Confidentiality
Processor personnel with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Security Controls
Processor maintains technical and organizational controls, including access control, encryption in transit, and audit logging.

6. Subprocessors
Processor may engage subprocessors and will maintain an up-to-date subprocessor list.

7. Assistance
Processor will assist Controller with data subject requests and compliance obligations as required by applicable law.

8. Breach Notification
Processor will notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

9. Deletion and Return
Upon termination, Processor will delete or return personal data unless retention is legally required.

10. Audit Cooperation
Processor will provide reasonable information to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.
