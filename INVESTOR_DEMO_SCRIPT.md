# ComplyAI Investor Demo Script (5 Minutes)

## 0:00 - 0:30 | Problem

"Compliance review is still manual and expensive. Startups copy templates, miss critical obligations, and only find problems during enterprise security reviews or diligence."

## 0:30 - 1:10 | Product One-Liner

"ComplyAI helps teams detect legal and regulatory risk in contracts and policies before customers, auditors, or investors do."

## 1:10 - 2:30 | Live Flow

1. Open `/upload`.
2. Select profile `multi` (GDPR + SOC2 + PCI).
3. Upload one sample startup document from `data/samples/startup_ai/`.
4. Click Analyze.
5. Open generated report.

## 2:30 - 3:45 | Show Output

1. Show `Top 3 Risks` section and citation tags.
2. Show `Suggested Fix Diff` (original vs improved clause).
3. Open flagged clause accordion and citations.
4. Click `Export PDF` to show board/audit-ready output.

## 3:45 - 4:30 | Platform View

1. Open `/dashboard`.
2. Show trend chart, distribution, and recent reports.
3. Mention history and workspace model (`users -> documents -> reports`).

## 4:30 - 5:00 | Positioning

"ComplyAI analyzes documented commitments and regulatory alignment. It does not replace infrastructure audits or legal counsel. It gives teams faster, citation-grounded decisions and stronger drafts before human review."
